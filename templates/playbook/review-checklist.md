## Owner sign-off

Done by the owner in TryPost, post by post. Nothing here is delegated to Claude.

- [ ] It is something I would recommend or say this period.
- [ ] The project data was revalidated recently enough and matches the project.
- [ ] Every fact is one I can stand behind; single-source and conflicting ones are handled as I want.
- [ ] Nothing reads like someone else's text, even reworded.
- [ ] It sounds like <project>; read aloud, it sounds like a person, not a translation or a chatbot.
- [ ] Any question is specific and worth answering.
- [ ] Each network's version suits that network.
- [ ] The image is ours or has a rights record; the alt text reads well; the preview and crop look right.
- [ ] I reviewed the warnings.
- [ ] Then I schedule or publish it myself.
