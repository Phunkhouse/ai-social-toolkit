# Profile: content publisher

Blogs, newsletters, podcasts, magazines: projects whose product is the content itself.

## Typical pillars

| Pillar | Tier | Shape |
| --- | --- | --- |
| New piece | Core | The one idea of a new article or episode, in our words, with the link |
| From the archive | Optional | An older piece that is relevant this week, with the reason |
| Quote | Optional | A short line from our own piece, attributed |
| Question | Optional | A question the piece raised, worth answering |

## Source adapter

The feed (RSS, CMS API) for the period: title, publication date, URL, author, summary written by the project. Older pieces from the archive by tag.

## Extra checks worth adding

- The linked piece is published (not scheduled or draft).
- A quote matches the published text exactly.
- Guest authors approved how they are quoted or named.
- No reuse of third-party text quoted inside our pieces.

## Risks

The temptation is to summarise the piece in the post; a post should give one reason to read it, not replace it.
